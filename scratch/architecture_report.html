1. Executive Summary & Vision

Estudio Arkipelago is a unified, high-performance digital workspace and operations platform engineered specifically for architectural practices, structural engineers, construction contractors, and external clients. The platform eliminates fragmented communication channels by combining interactive drawing annotation, construction administration (RFIs, Submittals, BP 344 compliance), time tracking & HR ledgers, and tokenized client review portals into a single reactive system.

6. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Hierarchy

Role Access Permissions & Capabilities Restricted Boundaries
Partner / Principal Full studio oversight, fee proposals, contract billing in PHP (₱), HR approvals, global financial analytics. None (Super-Admin).
Senior Architect Project management, RFI reviews, submittal approvals, blueprint version control, task assignment. Confidential studio HR complaint logs & master financials.
Junior Architect Sketch drawing canvas, task deliverables, time tracking logbook, studio wall communication. Contract pricing, HR admin, and project deletion.
Contractor / Site Engr. Submission of RFIs, material submittal uploads, site progress photos for assigned project code. Isolated strictly to assigned project; zero access to studio directory, HR, or other projects.
External Client Guest Viewing project drawings, 3D renders, pin comment feedback, 1-click milestone approval. Scoped strictly to tokenized review URL. Zero access to internal studio operations.

7. Strategic Delivery Roadmap

Phase 1 (Immediate Next Step): Client Review & Approval Portal (/review/[token]) allowing clients to approve drawing revisions via WhatsApp/Email links with a legal audit trail.
Phase 2: Multi-user collaborative drawing cursors inside /sketch using Supabase Realtime Broadcast.
Phase 3: AI-powered building code compliance scanner (BP 344 / Zoning Setbacks) and automated meeting minutes task extraction.
